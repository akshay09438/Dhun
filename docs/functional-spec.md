# Prompt-DJ — Functional Spec (V1)

_What the app does, for the user, screen by screen. Approved during discovery on 2026-07-05. This is a living document — kept true to the code. The "how it's built" lives in [technical-spec.md](technical-spec.md); progress lives in [implementation-plan.md](implementation-plan.md)._

> **LATEST — 2026-08-18, evening (A SONG WITH A LOOSE BEAT NOW MAKES A MIX INSTEAD OF BEING REFUSED; branch `fix/a-wobbly-beat-still-makes-a-mix`).** The founder uploaded their own song, used it as the beat, waited four minutes and got _"This pair didn't come out clean, so it wasn't shipped."_ Their question was the right one: _"we have the hard rule that every song which goes in has to generate"_. **(1) THE RULE WAS BEING HONOURED AND WAS FIGHTING ITSELF.** "Always make a mix" forced the singing to lock tightly, bar by bar, onto their song's beat. Their song's beat drifts about **a tenth of a second every beat** — a live, breathing pulse rather than a machine one. The lock could not hold, so the app's final quality check refused to ship a vocal that would wander off time. One rule created exactly the thing the other rule then rejected, and the person got nothing. **(2) WHAT CHANGED: the app now checks whether a beat is steady enough to lock onto, not merely whether it has beats at all.** Their song had 107 of them, all wobbly, so the old check waved it through. When the beat cannot hold a lock, the singing is laid over the track instead of being pinned to it — **and the mix comes out**, which is the founder's own rule. **(3) THE HONEST COST, chosen deliberately over refusing:** on a wobbly song the vocal **floats** rather than sitting tight against the beat, a bit like singing over a live band instead of a click track. On a normal song nothing changes at all — the tight lock is exactly as it was, and that is guarded by its own test, because the lock is what stops every ordinary mix drifting. **(4) THE QUALITY CHECK WAS NOT TOUCHED AND WAS NOT WRONG.** It would have handed over a mix that wandered. **(5) PROVEN, NOT ASSUMED:** the exact pair that was refused now finishes with three different vocals, and the audio was handed to the founder to judge by ear — because "looser" is a sound decision and only theirs counts. **(6) THE SAME SONG ALWAYS WORKED AS A VOCAL** — that was measured too. A loose beat only matters when it IS the beat. _(Founder `/zuko:fix` 2026-08-18.)_

This supersedes the entry below, which lasted half a day.** The founder used the morning's version and hit the next wall straight away: _"when I click on one of them, the 'Add my' thing disappears."_ **(1) THERE IS NOW ONE BOX, NOT TWO.** `/grind` had `my_beat` **and** `my_vocal`, and once you filled the first, Discord's own app often stopped offering the second — which is Discord's behaviour, not something this app can reach, so the design stopped depending on it. **You attach one song: `my_song`.** **(2) YOU ARE NOT ASKED WHICH SIDE IT IS.** The song simply goes in. **(3) AND THEN IT APPEARS IN BOTH LISTS.** Founder's own words: _"under Choose the Beat, their song name is also shown. Under Choose Vocal, the song is also shown, and they can choose whatever they like."_ Your song sits at the **top of both dropdowns**, so you decide what it does at the moment you make the mix, like any other song. **(4) SO MIXING TWO OF YOUR OWN IS JUST PICKING TWO THINGS OFF A MENU.** Add one song, add another, then `/grind` — both are in both lists, pick one as the beat and one as the vocal. No catalogue song involved. **(5) AND IT STAYS THERE.** The second time you want that song there is no uploading at all: type `/grind` and it is already at the top. **(6) YOU ARE ASKED WHERE THE DROP HITS EVERY TIME YOU ADD A SONG — one question, once.** This is deliberately **not** the thing that annoyed you before. Back then a song was locked to one side, so asking a vocal about its drop was asking about something that could never be used. Now any song you add can be picked as the beat, so the answer always matters. _(Founder decision, 2026-08-18.)_ **(7) NOBODY ELSE EVER SEES YOUR SONGS.** They appear in your picker and no one else's — the same privacy rule as before. **(8) YOUR SONG IS NEVER HIDDEN BY THE LANGUAGE FILTER.** The vocal list filters by language; your own songs skip that entirely, because an upload's language is a default nobody asked you for, and a language tag once hid 103 songs. **(9) HONEST LIMITS, UNCHANGED:** five songs each, twenty across everybody, MP3 or M4A, 30 seconds to 8 minutes, 30 MB. **(10) AND THE DROP YOU TYPE IS NOW ACTUALLY SAVED** even for a song the app already had — it used to recognise the song, skip ahead, and quietly bin the time you had just typed. _(Founder `/zuko:build` 2026-08-18.)_

> **LATEST — 2026-08-16 (YOUR MIX IS SENT TO YOU NOW, NOT JUST SHOWN TO YOU ONCE; branch `fix/the-mix-always-reaches-you`).** **(1) THE PROBLEM, PROVEN RATHER THAN GUESSED.** A finished grind arrived on a **private card**, and a private card is a kind of Discord message that is shown once, to one person, and **stored nowhere at all**. Asking Discord directly for the two cards Aashwin lost, by their own ids, returns "not found" for both. Grinder had done everything right each time — built the mix, packed it, attached it, updated the card — and the moment he closed the app his music ceased to exist. **38 of the 39 grinds ever made were in that position**; exactly one had a permanent copy, because somebody had pressed 📣. **(2) WHAT HAPPENS NOW: Grinder sends the finished mix to you as a direct message**, the same private chat you would get from a friend. It is exactly as private as the card — nobody else can ever see it — but Discord **keeps** it, for good, on every device you own. You do not have to ask, remember a command, or be quick. Privacy was never what made a mix disappear; being a disappearing kind of message was. **(3) THE CARD STILL CARRIES IT TOO.** Two routes, on purpose: the card is where you are looking in the moment, the message is the copy still there next month. **(4) IF YOUR DISCORD REFUSES MESSAGES FROM PEOPLE IN THIS SERVER**, Grinder cannot send you one — nobody can override that, and it should not be able to. So it says so on the card, in plain words, and names the one setting to change. A silent failure there would look exactly like the bug being fixed. **(5) A HICCUP IS RETRIED ONCE**, because one bad moment from Discord should not cost you a song. **(6) NOTHING ABOUT DELIVERY CAN LOSE A MIX** — if every route fails, the mix is still recorded and `/mygrinds` will still fetch it. **(7) `/mygrinds` STAYS**, but it is now the floor rather than the plan: the founder's call was that a mix should reach you "by hook or by crook", not that people should go looking for their own work. _(Founder `/zuko:fix` 2026-08-16.)_

> **LATEST — 2026-08-15 (THE VOCAL STOPS ARRIVING AT THE VERY END ON LONG BEATS; branch `fix/best-parts-keeps-the-vocal`).** Founder, on their own Rapture × God's Plan mix: _"the vocals of God's Plan are not coming. It's coming at the very last."_ **(1) MEASURED, NOT GUESSED.** Drake was placed three times in the full 8-minute mix — 1:04, 2:56 and 7:28 — and the 3-minute highlight kept **4:48–8:00**, so the first two were thrown away and the third landed **2 minutes 40 seconds in**. **(2) THE CAUSE:** the highlight was chosen by ending just after the LAST moment anyone sings and reeling back three minutes. On your mix, Drake now arrives **6 seconds in** instead of 2m40s, with 36s of singing instead of 31s. **(5) NOTHING ELSE CHANGED, deliberately.** Where the vocal is placed, how the mix is arranged, the length of the highlight and the rule that a lyric is never cut in half are all untouched. The rest of what the app does: two chosen songs → **Make my mix** → a "Studying your songs" screen splits + analyzes both (instant for catalog songs) → **"Make my mix"** produces a **full DJ arrangement** — Song 1's beat running throughout, with Song 2's vocal **weaving in and out across the WHOLE song as an energy arc** (an instrumental intro, then a vocal moment in each third — early, middle, and a strong entry saved for near the end — with beat-only stretches between), tempo-locked, on the beat and click-free, with a real one-bar **beat-breath** and one subtle **filter sweep** before a big entry, and — for contrast — **Song 1's own vocal answering in a gap** so the two songs trade (never two voices at once). The vocal no longer clusters in the middle of a long song; it spans the track and finishes strong. On a song with shaky analysis it automatically plays safer (fewer moves). The mix screen **shows the arrangement** (a two-lane timeline: where each song's vocal is, in distinct colors), and **"Give me another take"** (Regenerate) produces a different valid arrangement; **Download** saves the WAV. (Live steering now plays the **whole mix** — the mix screen's **live player** plays Song 1's beat/bass/melody **and** Song 2's arranged vocal together, and you reshape it live: tap any of the four parts — **Beat · Bass · Melody · Vocals** — or type a command ("take the bass out", "remove the vocals", **"drop everything but the beat"**, "bring it all back"), and it lands **on the next beat** with a smooth fade, replying in DJ language. As the mix plays, the live player also shows **1–3 AI suggestion chips that change with the part of the song** (e.g. "Bring the vocal in", "Drop to just the beat", "Fade it out") — tap one and it happens on the beat; and **"fade away"** fades the whole mix out. The suggestions are worked out **once per mix** by the AI (with a sensible built-in fallback if the AI's unavailable), and your own taps/commands stay predictable (M5 Slices 1–3). ## Screen 2 — Studying your songs (the wait) ✅ built

- **Purpose:** honest progress while we split stems + analyze (~1 min first time, then cached).
- **On it:** friendly step checklist that ticks off in order — "splitting the vocals, drums & bass… finding the beat, key & structure… planning your arrangement". Reached automatically after **Make my mix**; the user presses nothing else. On any step failure: a plain-language message + a **Start over** button (never a crash or a dead end).
- **Order note (as-built):** stems are split _before_ analysis runs, because the beat/structure read uses the split vocal to find where the singer sings.
- **Edge:** low-confidence song (loose beat, unclear structure, non-Western key) → the planner then picks safer moves (see DJ Handbook Part 9 fallback ladder). _(Surfacing that note on this screen is a later polish.)_
