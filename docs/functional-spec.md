# Prompt-DJ — Functional Spec (V1)

_What the app does, for the user, screen by screen. Approved during discovery on 2026-07-05. This is a living document — kept true to the code. The "how it's built" lives in [technical-spec.md](technical-spec.md); progress lives in [implementation-plan.md](implementation-plan.md)._

## What the app does TODAY (as of 2026-07-07; M1–M5 built + the curated-catalog MVP pivot)

**MVP pivot (2026-07-07):** users no longer upload — they **pick two songs from a curated dropdown** on the Setup screen (Song 1 = beat, Song 2 = vocals), each pre-analyzed and hand-tempo-verified so pairs blend. The catalog is currently a **single-beat "Father Ocean shelf"**: one beat (**Father Ocean**, 122 BPM / key 10B) + four vocals chosen for **key + tempo** compatibility (**Don't Start Now**, **Der Lagi Lekin**, **Tujhe Bhula Diya**, **With You** — the last two are on-the-edge borderline, pending founder ear-test). Song dropdowns are role-filtered (beats vs vocals) and there is no prompt box. The clean-blend tempo band was widened ±8%→±11% to fit the two Bollywood vocals. Everything below describes the mix/steer engine, which is unchanged by the pivot; the upload route still exists but only for operator ingestion of new catalog songs.

**IN PROGRESS (2026-07-08, on branch `feat/house-bollywood-energy-sync` — NOT yet merged to main):** the **House × Bollywood judgment** — a big upgrade to how the mix is arranged, built and founder-ear-confirmed through Step 2 of a 5-step plan (see [house-bollywood-recipe.md](house-bollywood-recipe.md) + [house-bollywood-build-plan.md](house-bollywood-build-plan.md)). What it changes: (1) **energy-sync** — the Bollywood vocal's most powerful part now lands on the house track's real **DROP** (detected from the energy curve), not just the loudest spot; (2) the **produced drop, founder-confirmed** — a multi-bar filter+volume BUILD climbs into each drop, and (Step 3) it's now a real held-breath-then-hit: the beat audibly **lowers to just the drums** for a couple of bars first (bass and melody gradually recede, never an abrupt cut), then the **bass keeps declining all the way through** the build, only **slamming back in right on the drop** with the vocal — a genuine "before" and "after," never a moment where the music goes silent mid-song (the build itself now also skips any spot where Song 1's own recording has a natural quiet breakdown, rather than blindly building through it); (3) **BOTH vocals trade** — the house track (Father Ocean) is no longer stripped; it LEADS its own substantial sung passages in the gaps and keeps its vocal LICK ringing into each drop, while the Bollywood vocal owns the drops (one lead at a time; the app decides who leads, keeping the real passages and dropping the scraps; the new beat move never plays under Father Ocean's own vocal either); (4) the **natural hand-off** — when they trade, the outgoing vocal's own natural decay rings under the incoming one (no imposed fade), the incoming enters at full; (5) **echo throws** — each sung phrase's last word echoes into its own pause on the drops; (6) **beat-up** — once per mix, in the app's strongest beat-only stretch, the melody audibly ducks (a real decline, never abrupt) so the drums and bass visibly drive for a few bars — "the beat takes over," matching the sound of the live tap-button of the same name, now placed automatically; (7) **breakdown** — once per mix, in another energetic stretch, the drums and bass gradually fade to a low simmer for a stretch (leaving the melody/pad exposed — the "hold your breath" space), then the beat kicks back in — the classic breakdown-and-return. With this, all **four beat moves are built** (bass pull-and-slam, drop to just the beat, beat-up, breakdown). **(8) Vocal chops on the biggest drop — tried, then PARKED (founder decision 2026-07-09):** the idea was, on the mix's loudest drop, to take the hook's first syllable and re-fire it rhythmically for a bar ("dum-da-ra-dum") before the vocal continues. It passed its safety review (can't clip, crash, or break other mixes), but on the founder's ear-test the chop landed on a breath and the drop went dead — so it was parked. The machinery stays dormant in the engine for a future, smarter version (one that grabs a punchy syllable, not the raw first slice). A prerequisite bug was also fixed: 3 catalog vocals had been analyzed before their vocal was separated, so the app never detected their singing (short, mid-word-cut vocals) — now recomputed. This all lives in the arrangement brain + render engine; the screens/flow below are unchanged. **Hook-on-drop (DONE 2026-07-10, founder-confirmed):** the drop now plays each song's **signature hook** (the recognizable line — _"Dil ye bekaraar kyun hai", "Aankhein teri kitni haseen"_), not whatever is loudest; the other vocal spots get the setup (other parts). Hooks are marked per curated song (in `app/planner/hooks.py`) and confirmed by ear. **Good-parts window (DONE 2026-07-09, founder ear-confirmed on Anchor Point × Maula Mere):** every mix is now built on the beat song's best **~90 seconds (60–120s)** instead of the whole track — a window anchored on the beat's **main (loudest) drop**, starting on a low-density **cue point** (a phrase boundary at a breakdown/quiet spot, never mid-chorus), building up to the drop, with the signature hook landing on that main drop, then a short **wind-down outro** so the mix eases out instead of stopping cold on the hook. Long songs (7–9 min) now collapse to their tight good part instead of dragging empty beat-only minutes. If the app can't confidently find a main drop, it falls back to today's full-track arrangement — never worse than before. **→ DISABLED 2026-07-09 (founder decision, after ear-testing full-song vs windowed): the good-parts window is now OFF by default — every mix is the FULL song again, not the ~90s good part. The window machinery is kept behind a flag (`_GOOD_PARTS_WINDOW_ENABLED = False` in `plan.py`), dormant and still tested, so it's a one-line re-enable if wanted.** **Still to finish:** (1) **make the mixes less "plain"** — the founder's #1 note: the vocal needs more energy/"play" to match the beat, and long songs feel empty because only ~3 vocal entries are spread across many minutes (the next concrete fix: **scale the number of vocal entries to the song's length** so the vocal keeps coming back — see the handoff); (2) the AI taste layer (Step 5). _(NOT doing: a "phrasing" grid-snap — tried 2026-07-10 and reverted, it pulled vocals off the drops; muting a beat's own vocal — the founder confirmed the natural fade-underneath hand-off is correct, not clutter.)_ Full rules: [docs/mix-recipe.md](mix-recipe.md). **Phase 0 — the vocal chain (STARTED 2026-07-10, on branch `feat/house-bollywood-energy-sync`):** the founder's #1 quality gap ("the mixes sound plain") gets a real fix — a nine-stage vocal-processing chain (de-ess, EQ, warmth, reverb, duck-under-vocal, …) plus pitch-repair so clashing-key pairs can blend. **Slice 1 shipped:** every mix now DEFAULTS to the hand-written **rules** arrangement the founder's loved mixes came from — the AI arrangement (which the founder consistently disliked) no longer runs unless explicitly switched on; and each mix now records its key-fit for reference. The audible vocal-polish chain is built off-by-default and turned on only after the founder's ear-tuning week — planned in [docs/phase0-vocal-chain-plan.md](phase0-vocal-chain-plan.md).

**Update (2026-07-11):** (1) **All five catalog vocal donors now have hand-marked hooks** (from the founder's marking session), so every catalog vocal mix lands its real signature line on the drop — including the corrected Father Ocean × Der Lagi baseline. (2) **Vocals snap to the beat more robustly:** a beat-lock fix (`m6.4`) means a mis-detected bar in a hook no longer un-snaps the vocal's entry — fixed the "vocal enters a beat late" case (Tujhe), founder-confirmed. (3) **The auto-crop (~90s good-parts window) is parked for good** — it can't produce a valid mix on the real catalog (the drops-vs-hooks vocal overlap fails the quality check), so full-length mixes are the hero, confirmed. (4) **Beat-matched sets** (joining several two-song mixes into one continuous set with beat-aligned seams) can now be heard end-to-end via a founder-run CLI tool (`scripts/build_set.py`); there is still **no in-app /set screen** (deliberately — the founder tests sets by ear, not a screen). (5) A **second beat, "I Adore You,"** was added to the local catalog (⚠️ **local-only** — the catalog manifest is gitignored, so this addition lives only on the founder's machine, not in git; not yet a confirmed permanent catalog member). (6) The **vocal-polish chain tuning is underway** (off in the shipped app; tuned per-render in a sandbox): `saturate_wet=0.3` approved so far, six knobs to go.

**Update (2026-07-14): the vocal-polish chain is now ON in the app.** The tuning week finished — all seven knobs were dialed in by ear on a full set A/B and approved (grit, brightness, a touch of reverb, light ducking, light compression, a low-end cleanup, and gentle de-essing). So **every mix now runs through that vocal polish**, in both the finished **Download** and the in-app **Play/steer preview** (the preview now sounds like the download — it used to preview a plain, unprocessed vocal). The old note above ("off in the shipped app… six knobs to go") is superseded. Two smaller things: the Play screen now shows a **"Loading your mix…" indicator** while a mix is buffering, so you're never left wondering if it's working; and the vocal **hand-off is now guaranteed clean** — when the two songs trade their vocal, the outgoing voice is _forced_ to fade under the incoming one (a real crossfade), so it can never be two loud voices at once, on any pair (fixed 2026-07-14; previously the outgoing was only _allowed_ to ring and, on a song whose vocal didn't naturally fade, could briefly compete). Two more features landed **invisibly** (no change to what a user sees today): **pitch repair** — the app can now nudge a clashing-key vocal into key — is **built but switched OFF** (the current catalog has nothing for it to fix; it's a one-line flip when clashing songs are added); and **set-order scoring** silently records, every time a set is built, the order the app would recommend versus the one the operator chose (data to make it smarter later). Neither changes the mix, the screens, or the sound.

**Update (2026-07-14, later — MULTI-SET + the Play screen stripped; on branch `feat/multiset-ui`, NOT yet merged):** the app can now make a **continuous back-to-back set of up to TWO song-pairs**, and the Play screen was **cleaned up** to match. This deliberately **reverses two earlier calls** — the "V1 is strictly two songs / no in-app set screen" non-goal, and having live steering on the Play screen — both **founder decisions this session**. What changed, in plain terms:

- **Setup screen — an optional second set.** You still pick one pair (a beat + a vocal) exactly as before; if you ignore everything else, the flow is identical to today. But there's now a small optional **"Add another set"**: use it and you can line up a **second** pair, see both in a **running order** on the right, reorder or remove them, then **"Build the set."** It's capped at **two** sets on purpose (keeps the render time and file size down). Single-set stays the default, effortless path.
- **A real set gets built under the hood.** Each pair is rendered through the exact same mix engine, then the two finished mixes are **joined into one continuous track with a beat-matched seam** (reusing the shipped set-join engine that was previously only reachable from a command-line tool — now it has a screen). A pair too far off-tempo to fit is **dropped with a plain reason** and the rest still builds. _Real example built + heard this session: Father Ocean × Der Lagi into Father Ocean × With You — one 15-minute continuous set, the second joining on the beat at 7:22._
- **Sets are added on Setup only** — there is intentionally **no "add a set" during playback** (that would need live audio-splicing, a much bigger future job). A note on Setup says so.
- **The Play screen is stripped.** The live-steering controls are **removed** — no Parts buttons (Beat/Bass/Melody/Vocals), no suggestion chips, no "beat up", and **no typed command bar**. In their place, the left panel now shows your **set line-up** (which set is playing now, which is up next). The screen just **plays the finished mix (single) or the continuous set (two)** — play/seek/time/export, and "another take" for a single mix. **This retires V1 Feature 2 (live steering) from the app** — a deliberate founder call, made after being told twice exactly what it was.

The two paragraphs just below (Screen 3's live-steering description) and the "two features" list describe the pre-strip behavior and are **superseded by this note** for anything Play-screen and steering related; they're kept for history. The mix/set-building engine itself is unchanged.

The rest of what the app does: two chosen songs → **Make my mix** → a "Studying your songs" screen splits + analyzes both (instant for catalog songs) → **"Make my mix"** produces a **full DJ arrangement** — Song 1's beat running throughout, with Song 2's vocal **weaving in and out across the WHOLE song as an energy arc** (an instrumental intro, then a vocal moment in each third — early, middle, and a strong entry saved for near the end — with beat-only stretches between), tempo-locked, on the beat and click-free, with a real one-bar **beat-breath** and one subtle **filter sweep** before a big entry, and — for contrast — **Song 1's own vocal answering in a gap** so the two songs trade (never two voices at once). The vocal no longer clusters in the middle of a long song; it spans the track and finishes strong. On a song with shaky analysis it automatically plays safer (fewer moves). The mix screen **shows the arrangement** (a two-lane timeline: where each song's vocal is, in distinct colors), and **"Give me another take"** (Regenerate) produces a different valid arrangement; **Download** saves the WAV. (Live steering now plays the **whole mix** — the mix screen's **live player** plays Song 1's beat/bass/melody **and** Song 2's arranged vocal together, and you reshape it live: tap any of the four parts — **Beat · Bass · Melody · Vocals** — or type a command ("take the bass out", "remove the vocals", **"drop everything but the beat"**, "bring it all back"), and it lands **on the next beat** with a smooth fade, replying in DJ language. As the mix plays, the live player also shows **1–3 AI suggestion chips that change with the part of the song** (e.g. "Bring the vocal in", "Drop to just the beat", "Fade it out") — tap one and it happens on the beat; and **"fade away"** fades the whole mix out. The suggestions are worked out **once per mix** by the AI (with a sensible built-in fallback if the AI's unavailable), and your own taps/commands stay predictable (M5 Slices 1–3). In the live player the **Vocals part is the arranged, per-bar beat-locked vocal** (the same vocal as the Download) — reverted from the earlier "continuous" experiment because it drifted; so the live player now sounds like the finished mix. The transport bar is **click/drag-seekable** and pause/resume keeps its place. The live player also has a **"Beat up"** energy move — tap it (or type "beat up") and the melody + vocals duck so the drums & bass drive the track (the beat takes over), on the next beat; "bring it all back" restores the full mix. The live player is a steerable _approximation_; the **Download stays the polished master** (it keeps the arranged vocal arc, the filter sweep, and the beat-breath). Still to come: final mastering and short-clip export. **Making a mix is now one click:** drop two songs → **Make my mix** → a "Studying your songs" screen splits and analyzes both automatically (an honest checklist: splitting the parts, finding the beat, planning the arrangement) → the finished mix. The old manual per-song **Split**/**Analyze** buttons are gone. **The whole app now wears its final look — the "Electric Violet" design (grey + one violet accent, Instrument Serif + Space Mono) laid out as four distinct screens (Setup → Generating → Play & Steer → Export) in a console/stage split**, and every mix gets a **playful AI-generated name** (e.g. "Ocean Bina") instead of "Untitled Mix". Still to build: the 14-second shareable clip export and final mastering (M6).) The sections below describe the full V1 target.

## The core value, in one sentence

A person who can't DJ picks two songs, and gets back a mix that sounds like a real DJ made it — then reshapes it with plain words.

## Who it's for

Casual music fans and creators who can't DJ but want to make good-sounding mashups to share or enjoy. First target slice: people making short mashup clips for TikTok / Reels / for fun.

## The two features (frozen scope)

1. **The Mix (offline):** two picked catalog songs — Song 1 (beat) + Song 2 (vocals) — become one finished, continuous track with Song 2's vocals arranged _like a DJ_ over Song 1's instrumental (vocals enter on drops/choruses, the beat sometimes drops out for a breath, Song 1's own vocal sometimes kept for contrast, everything on the beat). Not a flat paste.
2. **Instant Changes (live) + Regenerate:** while the mix plays, short typed commands land on the next beat; a Regenerate button produces a different valid take.

## Screen 1 — Pick your two songs ✅ built (curated catalog — MVP pivot 2026-07-06)

- **Purpose:** get the raw material in — **from a curated catalog, not uploads** (founder decision 2026-07-06: users kept hitting "tempos too far apart" from misread/incompatible uploads, so the MVP preloads hand-verified songs where **every pairing blends by construction**).
- **On it:** two song slots — "Song 1 → its beat" and "Song 2 → its vocals". Clicking a slot opens a **dropdown of the preloaded songs for that role only** — Song 1 lists only beat songs, Song 2 only vocal songs (filtered by each catalog entry's `role_hint`). Each song is pre-analyzed and tempo-verified once by us. **No prompt box** (it steered nothing at mix time — live steering happens on the Play screen, so it was removed to keep Setup clean).
- **Main action:** pick two songs → **Mix it**. _(2026-07-14: an optional **"Add another set"** lets you stack a **second** pair — capped at two — to play back-to-back as one continuous set; the running order shows on the right, reorderable/removable. Ignore it and this screen is unchanged. Sets can only be added here, not during playback.)_
- **Wider tempo reach now (movable master, 2026-07-08):** the app no longer needs both songs at nearly the same speed. When a pair is too far apart to force one onto the other, it meets them at a shared tempo — nudging the house track a little (protected: it barely moves, never dragged down) while the guest vocal takes the rest. This unblocked the founder's #1 pair, **Father Ocean × Tere Bina** (~143 vs 122 BPM, previously declined), now in the catalog and mixable.
- **Empty / error:** an empty catalog says "No songs in the library yet"; a failed catalog load says so plainly. No file-type errors anymore — there are no uploads.
- _(The upload flow still exists in the backend — it's how the operator ingests new catalog songs — but users never see it. "Uploads only" from the V1 table is superseded by this pivot for the validation MVP; open uploads may return post-validation, ideally with the tempo-fix control.)_

## Screen 2 — Studying your songs (the wait) ✅ built

- **Purpose:** honest progress while we split stems + analyze (~1 min first time, then cached).
- **On it:** friendly step checklist that ticks off in order — "splitting the vocals, drums & bass… finding the beat, key & structure… planning your arrangement". Reached automatically after **Make my mix**; the user presses nothing else. On any step failure: a plain-language message + a **Start over** button (never a crash or a dead end).
- **Order note (as-built):** stems are split _before_ analysis runs, because the beat/structure read uses the split vocal to find where the singer sings.
- **Edge:** low-confidence song (loose beat, unclear structure, non-Western key) → the planner then picks safer moves (see DJ Handbook Part 9 fallback ladder). _(Surfacing that note on this screen is a later polish.)_

## Screen 3 — Your mix (the heart)

> **⚠️ Superseded by the 2026-07-14 multi-set note above (branch `feat/multiset-ui`).** As of that change the Play screen has **no live steering** (Parts / chips / beat-up / command bar all removed) and instead shows the **set line-up** and plays the finished mix or the continuous two-set. The reshape-it-live description below is the pre-strip behavior, kept for history.

- **Purpose:** play the mix, show what the "DJ" decided, let the user reshape it.
- **On it:** play/pause; a simple timeline showing where Song 2's vocal enters and where the beat drops out (arrangement is visible, not a black box); the prompt/command bar; a **Regenerate ("give me another take")** button; an **Export** button.
- **Main actions:** play/pause; type a live command; regenerate; export.
- **Live command set (V1, lean):** "beat up" (drums/energy up), "take the bass out", "remove / bring back song two's vocals", "drop everything but the beat", "fade away". Each lands on the next beat/bar and answers back in DJ language ("dropping song two's vocal on the next bar").
- **Error / edge:** out-of-scope request ("add a third song", "make it sound like Drake", "change the lyrics", live BPM change) → polite decline that points to what V1 can do; a command with no clean runway → it waits for the next beat or does the closest clean move, and says so.

## Screen 4 — Export / share

- **Purpose:** get the mix out.
- **On it:** download the full mix, and a **short 15–30s clip** (the creator's native unit).

## Global
