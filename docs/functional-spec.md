# Prompt-DJ — Functional Spec (V1)

_What the app does, for the user, screen by screen. Approved during discovery on 2026-07-05. This is a living document — kept true to the code. The "how it's built" lives in [technical-spec.md](technical-spec.md); progress lives in [implementation-plan.md](implementation-plan.md)._

> **LATEST — 2026-08-12 evening (skip moves INSIDE a set, a language switch, and the new logo).** **Nothing about how a mix SOUNDS changed.** **(1) `/skip` now moves to the NEXT TRACK inside a set** rather than throwing all five away — a set is one continuous file, and the engine already knew where each track began; nothing had been using it. Past the last track it moves on normally. **(2) `/stop` remembers where you were, and `/play` picks up from exactly there.** `/stop` also no longer discards other people's queued mixes — it used to, meaning one person could bin everybody else's waiting grinds with nobody told why. **(3) `/play` exists.** Before it, the ONLY way to get Grinder into a room was to finish a grind while sitting in one; somebody who just wanted music had nothing to type. **(4) A VOCAL LANGUAGE SWITCH — "English" or "Bollywood".** The catalog is 14 Bollywood vocals to 4 English ones, so a listener in the US opened the picker and met a wall of songs they had never heard of. The picker now opens on English and switches at any tap. **It hides, it never blocks** — any beat still mixes with any vocal, so the cross-language pairs that make this app's best mixes (Father Ocean × Tere Bina) stay one tap away. Beats are never filtered; they are instrumental and belong to neither audience. **(5) `/help` was rewritten** — it still described "The Booth", a channel that has not existed since the rooms were split, and never mentioned the playback controls at all. Rooms are now live links that survive a rename. **(6) `/setup` is hidden** from everyone without Manage Server, so nobody can play with a command that restructures the server. **(7) Grinder's picture is the "# GRINDER" disc** everywhere it appears. _(Founder session 2026-08-12.)_

> **LATEST — 2026-08-12 night 2 (THE ROOM STOPS GOING SILENT, and the founder finally heard it; branch `zuko/goodnight-2026-08-12-night2`).** **Nothing about how a mix SOUNDS changed** — the mixing engine and the quality referee were not touched. **(1) ⚠️ VOICE IS NO LONGER A CLAIM. The founder ran `/grind`, sat in `#Bollywood_House`, and heard a real mix play.** Every earlier note in this document hedging that voice was "proven but not heard" is now settled: it works. **(2) The bug that made it look broken anyway.** Before that could be tested, every `/grind` answered _"The application did not respond"_ — because the bot was never running. `Start-Grinder.bat` contained an `echo` with a bare bracket in it (`(best-effort)`), and Windows reads a whole block before running any of it, so the launcher died straight after step 1 and never reached the line that starts the bot. The line was on a branch that **never runs on a machine that already has its setup** — an unreachable line killing the whole script. Fixed, plus six tests that fail if it recurs, including one that catches the launcher telling people to type a command the bot does not have (it was saying `/mix`, which does not exist). **(3) A listening room now keeps playing.** It used to play one grind and go **silent**, with the bot still sitting in the channel, until the last person left. Now when the queue empties the room becomes a **station**: it replays what the community has already made, favouring the mixes people gave 🔥. Walking into a quiet room starts it. **The bot still never judges a mix** — the ordering uses the community's own votes, is never announced, never shown, and never hinted at. A fresh grind always outranks a replay. **(4) `/skip` and `/stop`, usable by anyone in the room.** A bad mix whose owner has wandered off used to hold the room for three minutes. Not owner-only and not a skip-vote — at this size, being in the room is enough. `/stop` genuinely stops: it parks the station instead of letting it restart itself. **(5) Grinder now records who listened and for how long** — arrivals, departures, time in room. These are the two gaps recorded as blocking the community phase, and they were unmeasurable while rooms were quiet most of the time. **(6) Ten cards in one channel no longer shout over each other.** Each card was already polite on its own, but Discord limits edits per channel and the cards knew nothing about each other. They now share one budget; a skipped progress update costs nothing because the next one knows more. Your mix is never affected. **(7) The disk now tidies itself before things break, not after** — see the technical spec. _(Founder `/zuko:goodnight` 2026-08-12.)_

Now at most eight are made at a time and the rest wait in a line and are served in turn. Measured on the real engine: **20 grinds fired at the same instant, 20 finished, none failed.** This is not "one at a time" — mixes still overlap heavily; the limit only stops the machine biting off more than it can chew. **(3) A mix that fails now says WHICH kind of failure it was.** Until now, four completely different problems all showed the same sentence — _"Couldn't build this mix. Try another pair"_ — including when the laptop had simply run out of room, which has nothing to do with anybody's song choice. It now shows what is actually happening ("mixing it down", "checking it sounds right") and, when there is a queue, **"6 ahead of you — about 3 min"**. **(6) One person can no longer take over.** Someone firing ten grinds holds at most two at a time, so a newcomer's first grind never waits behind an enthusiast's tenth. **(7) Groundwork for several live rooms at once** — one Grinder can only be in ONE room at a time on the whole server, so extra rooms are silent rooms. The machinery for extra Grinder identities (free from Discord) is built and tested, though not yet connected to playback. _(Founder `/zuko:goodnight` 2026-08-12.)_

> **LATEST — 2026-08-11 (the Grinder Discord community becomes a real place; branch `feat/grinder-experience`, PR #29, merged as `d3f44a8`).** The Discord bot stopped being a command you type and became somewhere you hang out. **Nothing about how a mix SOUNDS changed** — the engine was not touched. **(1) Three commands, not five.** `/grind`, `/mygrinds`, `/help`. `/set` and `/songs` are gone. **`/grind` now takes no options at all** — it used to offer two blank "beat" and "vocal" boxes, and a first-timer reading two blanks cannot tell that leaving them empty is the right move. Type it, press enter, a picker opens. **(2) You stack your songs up front.** In the picker you choose a beat and a vocal, hit **➕ Add another pair**, and repeat up to five; **Grind it** stitches the lot into one continuous set. A briefly-shipped "➕ Keep going" button on a _finished_ grind was **removed as dishonest**: the engine cannot add a track to something that already exists, so pressing it rebuilt everything and swapped the audio out — which, mid-playback in a listening room, would have replaced what people were hearing. **(3) The bot never judges a mix.** A planned "flavour line" (_"these two have no business together"_) and verdict (_"clean grind" / "rough grind"_) were **cancelled before being built** — an opinion on the card tells people what to think before they hear it, and it poisons the reaction data, which is the actual signal. No tempo, no key, no mixing style, no take number either. A test now reads every card and fails on evaluative or technical wording. **(4) 🔥 💀 😐 on every grind, recorded per person.** Deliberately reactions rather than buttons, because buttons stop responding after half an hour and yesterday's grinds are exactly the ones a newcomer scrolls. Un-reacting really removes the vote. **(5) 📌 Pin it** carries any grind — yours or anyone's — up to the showcase channel; pressing twice cannot post twice. **(6) The server was rebuilt** into START HERE / GRIND / TALK with every channel's copy written and pinned, `#feedback` folded into `#requests`, the second voice channel removed, and the **`@Session Crew` role deleted** (an opt-in role with no way to opt in). Retired channels are only ever deleted when **empty** — one holding real messages is kept and reported. **(7) Grinding is confined to the grind channels** or to someone sitting in a listening room; anywhere else `/grind` declines and links the right place, so generation stays public where people can see each other work. **(8) Listening rooms are a whole CATEGORY, not one channel** — every voice channel under it counts, so new rooms work with no config change. A grind made by someone sitting in a room plays out loud _in that room_, one at a time. **⚠️ VOICE DOES NOT WORK ON THE FOUNDER'S MACHINE** — see the technical spec; it needs an x86 host. _(Founder `/zuko:goodnight` + follow-up fixes 2026-08-11.)_

> **LATEST — 2026-08-10 (fix — mixes are no longer wrongly skipped; half-time pairs are flagged; branch `fix/k1-f0-referee`).** Three sets in a row came back **"skipped"** with a key-check error. Diagnosis: the pitch engine was **correct** (measured +1.91 / −0.99 / −1.00 st for asked +2/−1/−1) — the **checker** was wrong: it judged pitch by fingerprinting notes across the whole vocal, which fails on rap/whisper voices (Bad Guy, Panda), so it binned good mixes. **Fixes:** (1) the checker now **measures the singer's actual pitch** (falling back to the old method only when a voice can't be measured); (2) **never-refuse now covers key too** — if a shift can't be produced or verified, the mix ships the vocal in its **natural key** instead of being skipped; (3) the ±2 limit is enforced in the checker as well. **Also:** a **half-time pairing** (one song ~2× the other, e.g. Silence 143 × Panda 72) is now **flagged** in the ops view — it locks perfectly on the beat but the beat pulses twice as fast as the vocal, which can feel frantic; the mix is still made. Ear-trialled by the founder on Father Ocean × Bad Guy before applying. _(Founder fix 2026-08-10.)_

> **LATEST — 2026-08-09 (fixes; branch `fix/download-and-abrupt-vocal-fade`).** **(1) The "Download full mix" button now actually saves the file.** It used to look like it worked but silently save nothing (it grabbed the mix, then threw the file away a split-second before the browser could write it). Now clicking Download saves the `.wav`. **(2) Vocal lines no longer cut off mid-sentence.** This was the real cause of the "abrupt ending" complaint: the app chopped every vocal line at a fixed length, ignoring whether the singer was mid-word — so ~3 of 4 lines stopped mid-sentence. Now the beat's line **finishes its phrase** (same breath-detection) and then **fades out gently (~1.5s) instead of a hard 0.4s cut** — and where it runs into the incoming vocal, it **trails softly under** it, exactly as asked (measured on Faded × Dooriyan: the beat's line now sings ~2s longer and fades under Dooriyan instead of cutting). _(Founder fixes 2026-08-09. > **LATEST — 2026-08-08 (vocal-rich beat rule + fixes; branch `feat/vocal-rich-beat-rule`, committed + pushed, PR open, NOT merged; live on localhost).** Some catalog beats are also full vocal songs (Faded, Lean On, Wake Me Up, Closer). Now, when you use one as **Song One (the beat)**, it **sings its own best line first** (a hand-marked "guest verse"), then **hands the mic to the vocal song** for the rest — like two singers sharing one mic, one voice at a time. ## Screen 2 — Studying your songs (the wait) ✅ built

- **Purpose:** honest progress while we split stems + analyze (~1 min first time, then cached).
- **On it:** friendly step checklist that ticks off in order — "splitting the vocals, drums & bass… finding the beat, key & structure… planning your arrangement". Reached automatically after **Make my mix**; the user presses nothing else. On any step failure: a plain-language message + a **Start over** button (never a crash or a dead end).
- **Order note (as-built):** stems are split _before_ analysis runs, because the beat/structure read uses the split vocal to find where the singer sings.
- **Edge:** low-confidence song (loose beat, unclear structure, non-Western key) → the planner then picks safer moves (see DJ Handbook Part 9 fallback ladder). _(Surfacing that note on this screen is a later polish.)_
